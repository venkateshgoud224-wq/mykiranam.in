PhonePe Payment Gateway Integration Summary

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Business Model

MyKiranam is a local kirana marketplace platform where customers can place orders from nearby kirana stores, receive quotations from sellers, make payments online, and collect their orders directly from the store.

Purpose of PhonePe Payment Gateway

We require PhonePe Payment Gateway for:

  • Secure online payment collection.
  • Dynamic QR generation.
  • Automatic payment verification.
  • Real-time payment status updates.
  • Webhook integration.
  • Refund processing.
  • Transaction tracking and reconciliation.
  • Eliminating manual screenshot verification.

PhonePe Integration Flow

  1. Customer places an order.
  2. Seller reviews the order and provides the final quotation.
  3. Customer makes payment through PhonePe.
  4. MyKiranam receives payment confirmation through Webhooks and Status APIs.
  5. Seller receives payment confirmation.
  6. Seller prepares and packs the order.
  7. Customer receives an "Order Ready" notification.
  8. Customer visits the store and picks up the order.

Delivery Model

Currently, MyKiranam follows a Self-Pickup Model.

  • No delivery service is provided in the MVP phase.
  • Customers collect orders directly from the selected kirana store.
  • Sellers prepare orders before customer arrival.

Payment Settlement Model

  1. Customer pays through PhonePe Payment Gateway.
  2. Funds are settled to the MyKiranam merchant account as per PhonePe settlement policies (T+1 or T+2 working days).
  3. MyKiranam reconciles successful transactions.
  4. Seller-wise settlement reports are generated.
  5. MyKiranam transfers seller payments to their registered bank accounts.
  6. MyKiranam charges a 1% platform commission on successful sales.
  7. Remaining amount is settled to the seller.

Example:
Seller Sales = ₹10,000
Platform Commission (1%) = ₹100
Seller Settlement = ₹9,900

Customer Cancellation Policy

To reduce fake orders and repeated cancellations:

First 3 Cancellations

  • No penalty is charged.
  • Warning notifications are sent.
  • Full refund is provided for paid orders.
  • Refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Refundable Customer Commitment Deposit

After 3 cancellations:

  • Customer must maintain a Refundable Customer Commitment Deposit.
  • Deposit Amount = 10% of order value.
  • Maximum Deposit = ₹50.
  • Deposit remains active for future orders.
  • Deposit is not consumed after successful orders.
  • Deposit remains valid for the customer's lifetime unless forfeited or refunded.

Deposit Forfeiture

If a customer cancels an order after seller preparation or does not pick up the prepared order:

  • ₹30 is compensated to the seller.
  • ₹20 is retained by the platform.
  • Deposit balance becomes zero.

To continue ordering, the customer must again maintain a new Refundable Customer Commitment Deposit.

Deposit Refund

If the customer permanently stops using MyKiranam:

  • Customer can raise a support ticket.
  • Refund request will be reviewed.
  • Approved refunds will be credited back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
