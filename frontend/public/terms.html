5. Delivery Model

Currently, MyKiranam follows a Self-Pickup Model. We do not provide delivery services in the MVP phase. Customers collect their orders directly from the selected kirana store. Sellers prepare orders before customer arrival, reducing waiting time and improving customer experience.

Future versions may support: Store-managed delivery and Third-party delivery partner integration.

6. Customer Cancellation Policy

To reduce fake orders and repeated cancellations, MyKiranam follows the policy below.

First 3 Cancellations

  • No penalty will be charged.
  • Warning notifications will be sent for each cancellation.

These rules apply to:

  • Manual Payment Orders.
  • Paid Orders.
  • Orders cancelled after the seller has prepared the order and marked it as Ready for Pickup.

Refund During First 3 Cancellations

If the customer has already paid for the order, the full order amount will be refunded. Approved refunds will be credited back to the customer's original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Refundable Customer Commitment Deposit

After 3 cancellations, customers must maintain a Refundable Customer Commitment Deposit before placing new orders.

Deposit Rules

  • Deposit Amount = 10% of the order value.
  • Maximum Deposit = ₹50.
  • The deposit remains in the customer's commitment balance.
  • The deposit is not consumed after successful orders.
  • The commitment balance remains valid for the customer's lifetime unless forfeited or refunded.
  • The deposit remains active for future orders.

Deposit Forfeiture Rules

If a customer cancels an order or fails to pick up an order after the seller has already prepared it:

  • ₹30 will be compensated to the seller for the loss and effort incurred.
  • ₹20 will be retained by the platform towards operational and support costs.
  • The customer's commitment deposit balance becomes zero.

To continue placing future orders, the customer must again maintain a new Refundable Customer Commitment Deposit.

Deposit Refund Policy

If the customer permanently stops using MyKiranam and their commitment deposit has not been forfeited:

  • The customer can raise a support ticket.
  • The refund request will be reviewed.
  • Upon approval, the unused Refundable Customer Commitment Deposit will be credited back to the customer's original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Objective: The objective of this model is to reduce fake orders, prevent repeated cancellations, protect local kirana stores from losses, improve order completion rates, and create a reliable marketplace for both customers and sellers.

7. Payment Settlement Flow

For online payments made through the PhonePe Payment Gateway:

  1. The customer pays through the MyKiranam platform using PhonePe.
  2. The payment amount is received and settled to the MyKiranam merchant account as per PhonePe's settlement cycle and policies (T+1 or T+2 working days, depending on the applicable settlement schedule).
  3. Once the settlement is received, MyKiranam will manually reconcile all successful orders.
  4. At the end of the day, seller-wise sales reports will be generated.
  5. MyKiranam will transfer the seller's payable amount directly to the seller's registered bank account.
  6. MyKiranam will charge only a 1% platform commission on the seller's total successful sales.
  7. The remaining amount will be settled to the seller.
  8. Settlement records will be maintained for transparency and reconciliation purposes.

Example

  • Seller Total Sales for the Day: ₹10,000
  • Platform Commission (1%): ₹100
  • Amount Settled to Seller: ₹9,900

Settlement Policy

  • Seller settlements will be processed after successful receipt of funds from the payment gateway.
  • Settlement timelines may depend on the payment gateway's settlement cycle, bank processing times, weekends, and public holidays.
  • Sellers will be able to view their sales and settlement information through the MyKiranam platform.

This model allows MyKiranam to securely manage customer payments, perform reconciliation, handle refunds when applicable, and ensure accurate settlements to sellers.
