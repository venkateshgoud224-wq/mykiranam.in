Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: June 04, 2026

1. Cancellations

Orders can only be cancelled before they are accepted and packed by the respective local seller. Once an order is marked as "Packed" or "Out for Delivery", it cannot be cancelled through the platform.

2. Refunds

If an order is successfully cancelled before processing, the refund will be initiated immediately. Please allow 5-7 business days for the amount to reflect in your original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, or UPI).

3. Defective or Incorrect Items

If you receive defective or incorrect items, you must contact the local seller directly upon delivery. Refunds or replacements for such issues are strictly at the discretion of the individual Kirana store owner.
